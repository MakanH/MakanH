## 🚀 About Me

I am a Computer Science Technology student at John Abbott College focused on blue-team security: building detections, then attacking my own lab to prove they work. I built a SOC home lab with a pfSense-segmented network and a Wazuh SIEM across Windows Active Directory and Linux, with hand-written decoders and correlation rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. I also built "Automated Threat Triage," a SOAR-style Python pipeline that pulls IOCs from phishing emails, enriches them through VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB, and explains every verdict it reaches. I am passionate about cybersecurity and about understanding how systems break in order to protect them.
